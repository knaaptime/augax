augax¶

Native draws for conditionally Gaussian augmentation in JAX. Each sampler runs as an XLA FFI custom call inside jit, vmap, lax.scan and pmap, with no host round trip.

import jax, augax

@jax.jit
def omega_update(z, key):
    return augax.pg_sample(1.0, z, key)

Each has a *_numpy twin that takes a numpy.random.Generator and makes the same draws for host callers. pg_mean and pg_var give the Pólya-Gamma moments in closed form, stable at z = 0 and differentiable, for EM and variational updates.

Parameters broadcast against one another, and shape= sets the shape of the result. An invalid parameter draws NaN, so a diverged chain stays visible. Draws take the precision JAX is running in: float64 under jax_enable_x64, and float32 otherwise. The samplers compute in double precision either way.

Pólya-Gamma¶

Draws are floored at 1e-12, so that they are usable as precisions.

At large |z|, PG(h, z) is an inverse Gaussian. Its cumulant generating function is h (r - r') + h (log1p(exp(-2 r)) - log1p(exp(-2 r'))), with r = |z| / 2 and r' = sqrt(r^2 - w / 2). The first term is that of IG(h / (2 |z|), h^2 / 4), and the second is at most h exp(-|z|). One normal and one uniform therefore draw it for any h, and the other samplers need not handle large |z| at all.

Elsewhere pg_sample uses the representation of PG as an infinite sum of gammas, omega = sum_k w_k G_k with G_k ~ Gamma(h, 1) (Polson, Scott & Windle 2013). It draws the first K terms and replaces the rest with a shifted inverse Gaussian matched to their first three cumulants. The inverse Gaussian suits the remainder because the weights fall as 1 / k^2, which gives its Levy density the same x^(-3/2) behaviour near zero, and it is the same law the whole sum tends to at large |z|.

The characteristic functions of this approximation and of PG are both in closed form, which bounds the Kolmogorov distance between them with no sampling noise. K is set from h to hold that bound below 1e-6 at every |z|, and the test suite recomputes it. A sample of n draws detects a distance near 1.6 / sqrt(n), so 1e-6 needs about 3e12 draws at a single (h, z).

Small h is the costly end, because the remainder then has to span many scales. K stops at 128, near h = 0.03, and the bound no longer holds below that. Against a 512-term series, 16M draws detect no difference at h = 0.01. At h = 0.005 the distance is 2e-3, at omega near 1e-6, against a standard deviation of 0.01.

The test suite also checks every regime against the exact Laplace transform, E[exp(-t omega)] = [cosh(z / 2) / cosh(sqrt(z^2 / 4 + t / 2))]^h, on real draws. It reaches scales of omega far below the standard deviation, and needs no reference sampler.

The widely used polyagamma 2.0.2 is measurably biased in several regimes, as checked with 8M draws against the exact mean and variance:

  • Its alternate sampler (Windle, Polson & Scott 2014), 1 < h < 4, small |z|: up to 0.9% high in the mean and 5.5% high in the variance. Its right-hand envelope uses the constant (pi / 2)^(h / 2), where both its gamma proposal and the density’s tail carry (pi / 2)^h.

  • h < 1: the alternate method assumes h >= 1, and below it polyagamma runs up to 0.7% low in the mean and 1.2% low in the variance.

  • 4 < h < 8 with |z| <= 4: its saddlepoint approximation runs 2.2% low in the variance at h = 4.5 and under 0.5% low from h = 6.

  • h > 50: its normal has none of the law’s skewness, which is 0.27 at h = 51.

The Kolmogorov scale¶

A standard logistic disturbance is a normal scale mixture, eps | lam ~ N(0, lam^2) with lam = 2K and K Kolmogorov distributed (Stefanski 1991). ks_sample(u, key) draws lam given eps = u, which makes a logistic latent variable conditionally Gaussian: the propagated disturbance of a latent SAR logit, for one. The draw is exact. It proposes 1/lam^2 from an inverse Gaussian, IG(1/|u|, 1) (a chi-square at |u| < 1e-4), and accepts with probability S(v) = f(v) e^{v/2} <= 1, f the density of (2K)^2, evaluated lazily by the squeeze of an alternating series: the Kolmogorov series above v = 4/3 and its Jacobi transform below, where the first’s partial sums are not bounds (Holmes & Held 2006). The tests check it against the identity E[exp(-t^2 lam^2 / 2)] = pi t / sinh(pi t) and against the posterior moments by quadrature.

The Chinese restaurant table count¶

crt_sample(y, r, key) draws L = sum_{n=0}^{y-1} Bernoulli(r / (r + n)), the tables that y customers occupy at concentration r. With it, a negative-binomial dispersion has a conjugate gamma update (Zhou & Carin 2015), the companion to pg_sample at h = y + r. The draw is exact. It draws the large probabilities one at a time and thins the small ones by geometric jumps, so its cost grows with min(y, r) and hardly at all with y beyond that. Counts and tables are in floating point, integer-valued.

The generalized inverse Gaussian¶

gig_sample(p, a, b, key) draws from the density proportional to x^(p-1) exp(-(a x + b / x) / 2). On the log scale, centred at the mode, this density is log-concave for every parameter, so a single rejection sampler serves them all (Devroye 2014). b = 0 with p > 0 gives the gamma, and a = 0 with p < 0 the inverse gamma.

Threads and reproducibility¶

A key’s elements form one stream, or one stream per block of 65,536 if there are more. Any free thread takes the next stream, on XLA’s thread pool for native calls and on threads of their own for NumPy calls. Chains under vmap therefore parallelize as one long array does. Set AUGAX_NUM_THREADS to cap the threads.

A draw depends on the key and the number of elements alone. The thread count does not change it, and vmap(f)(keys)[i] equals f(keys[i]). As in jax.random, closing over one key under vmap gives every row the same draws, so split keys per chain. augax does not promise equal draws across builds or machines: its rejection steps compare the results of the math library, whose last bits vary by platform.

Platforms¶

The FFI target exists on CPU, where the point is to keep this cheap scalar work next to the rest of a JIT’d Gibbs sweep. On any other platform the same call compiles to a host callback instead, which makes the same draws at the price of the round trip.
